Established 1992

Experience that stays close to the work.

For more than three decades, Fuller Architecture has helped congregations, municipalities, educators, healthcare providers and businesses shape places built to serve their communities for generations.

Founded by John M. Fuller, AIA, the firm serves clients throughout North Carolina, Virginia and the Southeast from the historic Southern Bank building in downtown Mocksville. In 2025, longtime team member Kevin S. Marion, AIA, became principal as part of a deliberate succession plan. John continues to provide design leadership and institutional knowledge.

Our focused studio structure keeps experienced professionals directly involved from early planning through construction. That means clear accountability, disciplined coordination and personal service—with the technical rigor expected of a seasoned architectural practice.
